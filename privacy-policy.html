SheDrives Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

At SheDrives, accessible from https://evernew-pro.github.io/shedrives/ and our official Android application, one of our main priorities is the privacy and security of our students and verified female driving instructors.

1. Information We Collect

When you book driving lessons, create an account, or contact us, we may collect:

  • Personal Information: Name, phone number, and address/training area in Lahore.
  • Location Data: With your permission, we access your device location to provide live location sharing with your family during active driving lessons for safety purposes.
  • Device & Network Information: Internet connectivity status and general device logs to ensure smooth app performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To match students with verified female driving instructors.
  • To enable safety features like live lesson tracking for family members.
  • To communicate lesson schedules, learner permit information, and booking updates via WhatsApp or phone call.
  • To maintain our safety promises (SOS assistance and contact concealment until booking confirmation).

3. Location Data & Family Safety

SheDrives accesses location data solely to fulfill our safety commitment: allowing female learners to share real-time trip progress with trusted family members. We do not sell or share location data with third-party advertisers.

4. Data Security

We implement standard security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

5. Third-Party Services

Our app facilitates direct contact via WhatsApp and phone dialer for lesson coordination. We do not share your private numbers with unauthorized third parties.
